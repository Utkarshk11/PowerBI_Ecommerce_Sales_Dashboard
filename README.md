🛒 Utkarsh E‑Commerce Sales Dashboard
📌 Project Overview
This project is a Power BI dashboard designed to analyze e‑commerce sales performance across states, categories, customers, and payment modes. It provides actionable insights into revenue, profit, customer behavior, and product trends, enabling data‑driven decision making.

📊 Key Metrics
Total Sales Amount: 438K

Total Profit: 37K

Total Quantity Sold: 6K

Average Order Value (AOV): 121K

📈 Dashboard Highlights
State‑wise Sales: Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, and Delhi contribute significantly to revenue.

Category Analysis: Clothing dominates with 63% of sales, followed by Electronics (21%) and Furniture (17%).

Customer Insights: Top customers include Yogesh, Yohann, Yash, and Yaanvi.

Payment Mode Trends: COD (44%) is the most used, followed by UPI (21%), Debit Card (13%), and Credit Card (12%).

Profit by Month: Seasonal fluctuations with peaks and dips across the year.

Sub‑Category Profitability: Printers, Bookcases, Sarees, Accessories, and Tables analyzed for contribution.

🛠️ Tech Stack
Power BI – Interactive dashboards and advanced visuals

DAX – Custom measures for KPIs and profitability

Data Modeling – Structured tables for sales, customers, categories, and payments

🎯 Impact & Learning
This project demonstrates how business intelligence and visualization can transform raw e‑commerce data into meaningful insights. It is ideal for:

Data Analysts: Showcasing portfolio‑ready dashboards

Students & Learners: Hands‑on practice with Power BI and DAX

Businesses: Understanding customer preferences and optimizing sales strategies
